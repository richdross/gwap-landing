## Inputs to recover (do not invent)

## Agent workflow and deliverable contracts
1. Research: sources.md with source URLs, dates, claim-evidence matrix, counterarguments and uncertainty; no fabricated statistics.
2. Editorial: story-brief.md with target viewer, promise, narrative arc, exact approved script reference, evidence placement and call to action.
3. Video: director-review.md with shot-by-shot asset mapping, timing, narration/caption checks, transitions, audio presence and QC. MP4 only after approved assets recovered.
4. SEO: article-draft.md with search intent, factual source citations, descriptive title, meta description, slug proposal, internal-link candidates, FAQ where genuinely useful. No keyword stuffing.
5. Distribution: youtube-metadata.md, shorts-plan.md, social-copy.md. Drafts only until founder approval.
6. Analytics: baseline.json and 7-day/30-day scorecards tracking GSC indexed status/impressions/clicks/CTR, GA4 landing/referral events, YouTube impressions/CTR/retention/watch time and subscribers. Mark unavailable metrics as unknown, never zero.

## First story
Working YouTube title: The Internet Has a New Gatekeeper
Working SEO angle: How AI Browser Agents Could Change Website Discovery
Audience question: What changes when assistants navigate websites and choose links for users?
Research guardrail: distinguish demonstrated browser-agent capabilities from forecasts about traffic or ranking impacts.
Source examples to validate: official browser agent documentation, Google Search Central, original demonstrations, reputable analysis.

## Review gates
G0 source recovery and provenance; G1 fact-check; G2 founder approves video and article; G3 technical QA; G4 explicit publication approval; G5 baseline and reporting.
No publishing, email sending, social posting, deployments, or agent queue dispatch without G4.
Never expose credentials in artifacts.

## Acceptance test
PASS only when: (a) sources and claims are auditable; (b) video exists with audible approved narration, readable captions and coherent continuity; (c) article is ready for editorial review; (d) distribution drafts exist; (e) actual publication URLs and analytics observations are recorded after approval.
An indexable article does not guarantee indexing or rankings. A produced video does not guarantee reach.
